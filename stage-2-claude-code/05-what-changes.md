# 05 · What changes with Claude Code

**Goal:** understand the one big difference before you touch it. **Time:** 10 minutes. Nothing to install.

## Chat answers. Code acts.

In the Chat tab, Claude writes text and you do the work.

In the **Code** tab, Claude can open the files in a folder you choose, read them, change them, create new ones, and run small programs, while you watch. It is the same Claude. It just has hands now.

That is why it needs three rules from you.

## The three rules

1. **Work on a copy.** Never point it at your only version of anything. Copy the folder first.
2. **One folder at a time.** You choose the folder. It only sees that folder. Never choose your whole Desktop or your whole user folder.
3. **Plan first.** There is a mode called **Plan** where Claude can only look and propose. It cannot change anything. You will start every exercise there.

## What you will see

- A **mode selector** next to the send button. Plan, Manual, Accept edits, Auto. You will use Plan and Manual for now.
- A **stop** button and the **Esc** key. Both stop Claude immediately. Use them freely.
- A small **+12 -1** counter when files change. Click it to see exactly what changed, line by line.
- A **usage ring** next to the model name. Click it to see how much of your plan you have used.

## Keep this in your head

It will sometimes be wrong, confidently. Your job is not to type. Your job is to read what it proposes and say yes or no. That job stays yours the whole way through this path.

## You are done when

You can say the three rules from memory.
