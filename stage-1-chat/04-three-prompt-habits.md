# 04 · Three prompt habits

**Goal:** three small changes from how you prompt ChatGPT. **Time:** 15 minutes.

Everything you already do still works: clear goals, examples, asking for a format, iterating. Change only these three.

## 1. Say why, not just what

Instead of a rule, give the reason. Claude generalizes from the reason.

- Weaker: *"Never use bullet points."*
- Better: *"This will be read aloud on the phone, so write it as flowing sentences."*

## 2. Documents first, question last

When you paste a long text, put the text at the top and your question at the bottom. Anthropic measures up to 30% better answers this way.

## 3. Use the effort setting instead of "think step by step"

Click the model name, then **Effort**. Low or Medium for everyday things (they also stretch your usage further). High for something that matters. That replaces the "think carefully, step by step" boilerplate. Drop the ALL CAPS and the MUST / NEVER. Modern Claude over-reacts to shouting.

## The one test that matters

> Show your prompt to a colleague who knows nothing about the task. If they would be confused, Claude will be too.

## Try it

Take a prompt you used last week in ChatGPT. Rewrite it with the three habits. Run both. Compare.

## You are done when

You have rewritten one real prompt and seen the difference. **Stage 1 is complete.** You are a Claude chat user.
