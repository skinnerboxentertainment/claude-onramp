# 03 · Memory and privacy

**Goal:** know what Claude remembers, and control it. **Time:** 15 minutes.

## Do this

1. In any chat, ask: *"What do you remember about me?"* Read it.
2. Open **Settings > Memory**. Every item is a small note you can read, edit, or delete. Delete one you do not like.
3. Note the two switches: **Pause** (keep what exists, stop adding) and **Reset** (delete everything, cannot be undone). Leave them alone for now.
4. Open **Settings > Privacy**. Find "Help improve our AI models". Decide for yourself. Off means your chats are kept 30 days and not used for training.
5. Start an **incognito chat** (the ghost icon in the chat box). Nothing from it is saved or remembered. Use it for anything private.

## Optional: bring your ChatGPT memory with you

Settings > Memory > **Start import**. Claude gives you a prompt to paste into ChatGPT; ChatGPT writes out what it knows about you; you paste that back. Ten minutes. Skip it if you would rather start clean.

## Good to know

Claude skips sensitive topics (health, religion, politics) unless you tell it to remember them. Each Project has its own separate memory.

## You are done when

You have seen your memory list and know where the ghost icon is.
