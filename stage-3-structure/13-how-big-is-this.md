# 13 · How big is this?

**Goal:** size a task before you start, so it fits. **Time:** 15 minutes, reading and one exercise.

## Three sizes

**Small.** You could describe the change in one sentence. *"Fix the typo in the title."* Skip the plan. Just say it, in Manual mode, and accept.

**Medium.** It touches several files, or you are not sure how. *"Reorganize these notes by month."* Run the loop: plan, read, approve, check. Everything in stage 2 and 3 was medium.

**Big.** It would take you more than an afternoon, or you cannot picture the finished thing. *"Build me a system for tracking all the vendors."* Do not start. Split it. Ask Claude: *"Break this into pieces I can finish one at a time. Each piece should have its own check."* Then do the first piece as a medium task. Stage 4 shows how to write the big ones down.

## The mistakes Anthropic sees most

These come from the official Claude Code guidance. Every one applies to you.

1. **The kitchen-sink session.** Three unrelated jobs in one conversation. Start a new session per job (**Ctrl+N**).
2. **Correcting over and over.** After two failed corrections, stop. New session, better request.
3. **The bloated `CLAUDE.md`.** Fifty rules, none followed. Keep it to five.
4. **Trusting without checking.** Page 12.
5. **Endless exploring.** *"Look into this"* with no end. Say what a good answer looks like.

Plus the opposite mistake: planning a one-sentence change. Do not over-plan small things.

## Do this

Write down three tasks from your real work. Label each S, M, or B. For the B one, ask Claude to split it, and note the first piece. Do not do it yet.

## You are done when

You have three labeled tasks and a first piece for the big one.
