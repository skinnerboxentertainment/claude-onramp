# 10 · The loop

**Goal:** the one habit that makes everything else work. **Time:** 20 minutes.

You already did this loop twice without naming it. Here it is, named.

## The loop

1. **Say the goal.** One or two sentences. What you want, and why.
2. **Get a plan.** Mode: Plan. Claude looks and proposes. Nothing changes.
3. **Read the plan.** Argue with it. Cut things. Add the one thing it missed.
4. **Approve.** Switch to Manual (or Accept edits when you trust it). Say "go".
5. **Check.** Open the result. Compare it to what you asked for. Not to what Claude says it did.
6. **Keep or undo.**

That is the whole method. Every page after this one is a refinement of one step.

## Do this, on something real

Pick a small thing from your actual work that involves files. Something you would do by hand in twenty minutes. Examples:

- Turn a folder of meeting notes into one summary per month.
- Take a spreadsheet and produce a short report in Spanish.
- Merge three versions of a document into one and list what differed.

Copy the files into a fresh folder. Open it in the Code tab. Run the loop. Do not skip step 2 or step 5.

## What to watch for

In step 3, the plan will often be bigger than you asked for. Cut it back. Claude is eager; you are the editor.

In step 5, the most common trap: reading Claude's summary of what it did instead of looking at the thing. Look at the thing.

## You are done when

You ran the loop on your own task, from your own idea, without a script.
