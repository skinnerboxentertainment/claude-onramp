# 11 · Interview me

**Goal:** turn a fuzzy wish into a clear plan, by letting Claude ask you questions. Then build a small tool you can use. **Time:** 45 minutes.

## The trick

When you do not know exactly what you want, do not guess. Say:

> Interview me first. Ask one question at a time until you understand what I need. Then write the plan.

Claude will ask better questions than you would think to answer.

## Do this

1. Make a new empty folder on your Desktop, for example `my-tool`. Open it in the Code tab. Mode: **Plan**.
2. Think of one small tool that would help you this week. A page that converts something. A checklist you keep re-typing. A calculator for a thing you calculate by hand. Keep it tiny.
3. Send:
   ```
   I want a single web page, one HTML file, that [describe it in one sentence]. Interview me first: ask one question at a time until you understand what I need. Then write the plan. Do not build yet.
   ```
4. Answer the questions honestly. Say "I don't know, you choose" when you do not care.
5. Read the plan. Cut anything you will not use.
6. Switch to **Manual**. Send: *"Build it."* Accept the file.
7. Open the folder in Explorer. Double-click the `.html` file. It opens in your browser. Use it.
8. Tell Claude what is wrong. *"The button is too small on my phone."* *"Put the total in bold."* Accept the change. Refresh the browser.

## Why this matters

You just went from a wish to a working thing without writing a line of code, and every decision along the way was yours. That loop, with the interview at the front, scales from a one-page tool to a month-long project. The only thing that changes is how long the plan is.

## You are done when

You have a page in your browser that does something you wanted, and you changed it at least once.
