# 14 · When it goes wrong

**Goal:** know the five moves, so a bad moment does not become a bad hour. **Time:** 10 minutes.

## The five moves

**1. Stop.** Press **Esc** or click the stop button. Claude pauses at once. Nothing is lost. Say what you want instead.

**2. Do not argue.** If you have corrected the same thing twice and it is still wrong, the conversation is polluted. Stop correcting. Open a new session (**Ctrl+N**), write a better request that includes what you learned, and start clean. This feels wasteful. It is the fastest route.

**3. Ask on the side.** Type `/btw` followed by a question. It answers without adding anything to the main conversation. Use it for *"what does this file do?"* or *"is this a bad idea?"*

**4. Check the usage ring.** Click the ring next to the model name. Two numbers: how full this conversation is, and how much of your weekly plan is used. When a conversation is nearly full, Claude gets forgetful. New session.

**5. Ask Claude to reverse.** *"Undo everything from the last request."* It remembers this session. Then check the folder.

## Things that are not emergencies

- It proposed something you did not ask for. Reject it. Say why.
- It says "done" and it is not done. Point at the check from page 12. *"You said the CSV matches. Row 7 does not. Look again."*
- It answered in English when you wrote in Spanish. Tell it once; put it in `CLAUDE.md` if it keeps happening.

## The one thing that is a real risk

Pointing it at your only copy of something and switching to Auto or Accept edits before you trust it. Rule 1, page 05. Everything else is recoverable.

## You are done when

You have used `/btw` once and started one fresh session on purpose. **Stage 3 is complete.** You can take a fuzzy goal, get a plan, approve it, and prove the result. That is the skill.
