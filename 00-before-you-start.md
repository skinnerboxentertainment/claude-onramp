# Before you start

**Time:** 5 minutes.

## Check three things

1. **You have Claude Pro.** Claude chat works on the free plan. Claude Code does not. Sign in at [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) and look for "Pro" next to your name.
2. **Install the Claude desktop app for Windows.** Download it from [claude.com/download](https://claude.com/download). Sign in with the same account. You will use this app for everything in this path. The website works for stage 1 too.
3. **Pick your language.** In the app, click your profile picture, then Language. Spanish (Latin America) or Spanish (Spain) if you prefer. Claude itself answers in whatever language you write, regardless of this setting.

## Two things Claude does not do

So you do not go looking:

- It does not make images or video.
- It does not remember a conversation unless memory is on (you will see this in page 03).

## You are done when

You see the three tabs at the top of the desktop app: **Chat**, **Cowork**, **Code**. You will only use Chat until stage 2.
